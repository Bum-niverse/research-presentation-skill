# Korean Research & Data Project Profile

Use this profile for Korean university research, data-analysis, machine-learning,
lab, and project presentations that must remain editable in PowerPoint.

## Typography

- Primary family: `Pretendard`
- Title weight: ExtraBold or Bold
- Section and card heading: SemiBold
- Body: Regular or Medium
- Caption and source: Regular
- Do not mix more than two Korean font families in one deck.
- Use `Noto Sans KR` only as a compatibility fallback when Pretendard is not
  available.
- Recommended minimums: cover 42 pt, slide title 28 pt, body 15 pt,
  caption/source 9 pt.
- Avoid narrow text boxes that cause Korean particles or short words to wrap
  alone onto the next line.

Font source:

- Pretendard: https://github.com/orioncactus/pretendard
- Noto Sans KR: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Noto+Sans+KR

## Visual Grammar

Default to an evidence-first light report system:

- white or very light gray canvas;
- ink/navy text;
- one blue primary accent;
- mint for positive response or growth;
- amber for caution or testing;
- red only for risk, failure, or exclusion;
- thin rules and open columns instead of repeated card grids;
- one dominant chart, table, or conclusion per evidence slide.

Recommended composition grammar:

- analysis/report: `Evidence Plate`;
- implementation/project update: `Operating Grid`;
- model monitoring: `Telemetry Canvas`.

## Slide Sequence

Prefer this cumulative narrative for research and data projects:

1. Problem and decision context
2. Dataset and analysis boundary
3. Main evidence
4. Target or hypothesis
5. Strategy or model design
6. Experiment and measurement
7. Expected impact, risk, and next action

Each title should state the takeaway rather than only naming the section.

## Data Presentation

- Show the denominator or sample size for rates.
- Separate observed evidence from proposed action.
- Mark post-event variables that cannot be used in pre-event prediction.
- Avoid claiming causality from descriptive group rates.
- For A/B tests, define the audience, treatment, primary metric, guardrail,
  and decision rule.
- For machine-learning slides, distinguish training features, prediction
  timing, target label, evaluation metric, and operational action.

## Editing & QA

- Keep text, shapes, charts, and tables editable.
- Preserve speaker notes and source blocks.
- Render every slide after a font change because Korean line breaks can shift.
- Check actual PPTX font names, not only screenshots.
- Run overflow, placeholder, and visual consistency checks before delivery.
